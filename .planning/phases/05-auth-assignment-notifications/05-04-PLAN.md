<objective>
Teach the one module that owns ADR-008 a third outcome. Today `access.py` answers *the task* or
*not found*; Phase 5 needs *the task*, *not found* and *forbidden*, and the assignee is the first
actor in this project who can see a resource they may not change.

Purpose: AUTH-06 and D-01..D-03 are one rule, and eleven use cases call it. Putting the assignee
clause in the use cases instead would be eleven chances to get the 404/403 split wrong, which is
precisely what ADR-055 exists to prevent. Doing it before any Phase 5 use case is written means
`AssignTask` and `UnassignTask` are born with the correct guard rather than retrofitted.
Output: the assignee short-circuit in `visible_task`, `owned_task`, the rewritten docstring, and
`UpdateTask` / `DeleteTask` on the owner-only door.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
