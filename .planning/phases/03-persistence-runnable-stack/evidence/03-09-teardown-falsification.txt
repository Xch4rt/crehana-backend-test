03-09 Task 3 - falsification of test_the_dependency_does_not_commit_on_teardown
================================================================================

The claim under test is research Anti-Pattern 1 / ARC-08: `get_uow` constructs
the unit of work and enters its block, and never makes that block's work durable
in its own teardown. A green test alone cannot distinguish "the provider does not
do it" from "the test could not have seen it if it did", because FastAPI runs the
exit half of a `yield` dependency after the response has already been sent.

So the provider was broken on purpose and the test was re-run.

The only change, in src/taskmanager/presentation/api/dependencies.py::get_uow:

    async with SqlAlchemyUnitOfWork(get_session_factory(request)) as unit:
        yield unit
+       await unit.commit()

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    $ .venv/bin/pytest \
        tests/integration/test_dependencies.py::test_the_dependency_does_not_commit_on_teardown \
        --no-cov -q

        response = await probe_client.get("/_uow/write-without-finishing")

        assert response.status_code == 200
        assert response.json() == {"wrote": str(USER_ID)}

The row written by a handler that never asked for it was still there, read back
from a second, independent connection opened after the response had come back.
